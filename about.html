Frequently Asked Questions

How to use this portal?

Start at Choose Hazard to learn what to do before, during and after a disaster. Then fill out My Checklist, review the Kit Guide and write down your Family Plan.

What to do in an emergency?

Stay calm, get to a safe place and follow instructions from local officials. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.

Contacting authorities

Dial 911 for police, fire and medical emergencies. The Philippine Red Cross hotline is 143. For evacuation centers and local advisories, contact your barangay hall or city/municipal DRRMO.

What is a resilient community?

A community whose members know their risks, plan together and help one another prepare for, respond to and recover from disasters.

What should I keep ready at all times?

A Go-Bag with water, food, first aid, a flashlight and radio, copies of important documents, and a family contact list. See the Kit Guide.

Where can I get training?

The Philippine Red Cross, your local DRRMO and the Bureau of Fire Protection offer first aid, CPR and drill training. Ask your barangay for the next schedule.
