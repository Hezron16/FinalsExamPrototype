Emergency Kit Guide

Essential Go-Bag Supplies for Every Disaster.

Water & Food

1 gallon (about 4 liters)/person/day, 3-day non-perishables.

First Aid

Bandages, antiseptic, meds, gloves, mask.

Radio & Power

Battery or crank radio, flashlight, spare batteries, power bank.

Important Documents

ID, insurance, deeds, medical records in waterproof bag.

Hygiene & Sanitation

Soap, sanitizer, wipes, garbage bags.

Clothing & Shelter

Sturdy shoes, warm clothes, emergency blanket, poncho.
